What affects a used phone’s value?

  • Model and storage: Include the exact model and storage capacity so it can be compared with similar phones.
  • Condition and battery: Describe screen damage, scratches, battery health and any faults; photograph them clearly.
  • Locks and repairs: Disclose account or network locks, replaced parts and previous repairs.
  • Age and accessories: Say how long you have used it and which charger, box or accessories are included.
