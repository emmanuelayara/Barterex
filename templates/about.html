Who we are

Welcome to Barter Express, a modern trade-by-barter platform designed to bring smart, seamless, and fair exchange to today's world. We believe that value should not be limited to money alone, and that people deserve a safe, efficient, and transparent way to trade goods and services directly.

Our product

Barter Express is a digital cashless marketplace that transforms how people exchange goods. Instead of letting items gather dust or struggling with direct trades, Barter Express users get an AI valuation of their items, earn provisional BXC (Barter Express Credits), and use them to reserve other items on the marketplace. You keep your items with you until a trade is actually underway - we never take possession of your item just to value or list it. Items are physically verified only at the point of an exchange, at a pickup station or through an optional home service.

With Barter Express, you:

  • Upload photos and descriptions to get an AI valuation, while your item stays with you.
  • Earn provisional ᗸ BXC (Barter Express Credits) based on your item's estimated value.
  • Use BXC to reserve items on our marketplace, backed by your own items.
  • Have items physically verified at a pickup station (free) or through our home service (quoted fee).
  • Pay a 5% transaction fee (minimum ₦1,000, maximum ₦5,000) to the company at our office only when an exchange successfully completes.

Our goals

Our mission is to redefine trade in the digital age by making barter as easy as shopping online. We aim to:

Empower people

Extract value from what you already own and make every item count in your trading journey.

Reduce waste

Give items a second life by keeping them in circulation and reducing environmental impact.

Financial inclusivity

Create an alternative to cash transactions that's accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Build trust

Foster communities where fair exchange and mutual respect fuel everyday living.

How we achieve this

  1. Technology-driven platform

    Built with simplicity, security, and reliability at its core for seamless user experience.

  2. Fair valuation system

    Our AI-powered valuation engine analyzes item images and descriptions to provide accurate market value estimates. Our professional team reviews valuations, and items are physically verified before any exchange is finalized, so credits stay fair for all users.

  3. User-friendly experience

    From signup to trading, we ensure the process is simple, transparent, and rewarding.

  4. Community & feedback

    Active engagement with users during beta testing and beyond for continuous improvement.

  5. Growth & expansion

    Committed to scaling across regions and introducing features that make barter smarter.
