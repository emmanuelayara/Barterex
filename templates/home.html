How it works

Four simple steps to start trading items in our community.

Step 1

Upload your items

Create an account, update your profile and add your items with clear photos, descriptions and condition ratings. Your item stays with you.

Step 2

Get valued & earn BXC

Our AI estimates fair market value and our team reviews it. Accept the valuation to receive provisional Barter Express Credits (BXC).

Step 3

Browse & reserve

Explore the marketplace, use smart filters, and reserve the items you want with your BXC, backed by your own items.

Step 4

Verify & collect

Items are physically verified at a pickup station or through our home service. Once verification passes, the exchange completes.
