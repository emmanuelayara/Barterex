How do I create an account?

Creating an account is simple! Click the "Sign Up" button on the homepage, provide your email address, create a password, and fill out your profile information. You'll need to verify your email address before you can start using Barter Express.

How do I add items to my inventory?

Once logged in, go to your Dashboard and click "Add Item". Fill in the item details including name, description, category, and condition. Upload clear photos of your item - our AI valuation system analyzes the images and description to provide a market value estimate. Your item stays with you; it is only physically verified later, when you use it in an exchange. Your first valuation each day is free, and additional valuations cost ₦150 each.

Why isn't my item showing up in the marketplace?

Items move through valuation and activation-video review. Once an admin approves the video, your item is activated and appears in the marketplace. If your item hasn't progressed after 24-48 hours, check that you've provided the required information and clear photos, or contact support.

How is the value of my item determined?

Our AI-powered valuation system analyzes your item's images and description to estimate its fair market value. The system considers factors like item condition, brand, age, and current market prices. Our professional appraisal team reviews the AI valuation and may adjust it if needed. Your credits are confirmed when your item passes physical verification during an exchange, so they reflect true market value.

How does the trading process work?
  1. Add your item and get an AI valuation - your item stays with you
  2. Submit an activation video for admin review; once approved, your item is listed and provisional ᗸ BXC is added
  3. Browse the marketplace and reserve an item with your BXC, backed by your own items
  4. The owner brings the reserved item to your selected verification centre within 72 hours. After it passes, you have 48 hours to attend, complete any backing-item checks, accept the final terms, pay and collect.
  5. Once verification passes, the exchange completes and you collect or receive your item
  6. Confirm receipt and rate your exchange
How safe is trading on Barter Express?

We take safety very seriously! All users are verified and identity-checked before participating. Items are physically verified by our team before any exchange is finalized, and your item stays with you until then. There is no direct contact between users - exchanges are coordinated through the platform, with built-in protection mechanisms. This eliminates common risks associated with informal trading. Report any suspicious activity to our support team immediately.

How does the rating system work?

After completing a trade, both parties can leave a rating and review for each other. Ratings help build trust in the community. You can only rate after a trade is complete. Your rating history is visible on your profile to help others decide whether to trade with you.

Do I have to pay to use Barter Express?

Creating an account and listing items is completely free, and your first AI valuation each day is free. Additional valuations cost ₦150 each. New reservations have a service fee of 5% of the agreed value of the item you receive (minimum ₦1,000, maximum ₦10,000), shown upfront. The reserved item is verified first. You are then invited to attend the centre within 48 hours, bring any backing items for inspection, accept the final terms and pay by card or bank transfer through your order's Paystack link. Company inventory is already verified, so that window starts when reserved. The service fee is not deducted from BXC. Confirmed payment and satisfied BXC requirements are required before collection. Collection at the centre has no extra charge.

What if there's a problem with my transaction?

If you have an issue with a transaction, contact our support team immediately through the app. Document the problem with screenshots, photos, or other evidence and submit a support ticket. Our support team will investigate all issues, including collection problems, item condition mismatches, or technical issues. We work toward a fair resolution for all parties involved.

How do I collect my item?

Bring items to the selected verification centre when notified. The reserved item is inspected first; buyers using item-backed BXC then bring their backing items for inspection. After final acceptance, confirmed service-fee payment and BXC settlement, the buyer collects at the centre. You'll receive notifications at each stage.

Can I cancel a trade offer?

You can cancel a trade request or reservation while it's still pending, before verification begins. Once verification is underway, cancellation policies apply. Failing to submit items for verification within your applicable window can lower your reliability score. Contact support if you need to cancel a completed exchange.

How do I delete my account?

You can request account deletion from your account settings. However, if you have active or pending trades, you'll need to complete or cancel them first. Your rating history will remain visible to the community even after account closure to maintain trading history integrity.

Visit our Contact Us page to reach our support team. You can also check our About Us page for more information. We typically respond to inquiries within 24-48 business hours.
