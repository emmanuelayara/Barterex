What affects a laptop’s value?

  • Make and model: Give the model and, if known, the generation or year.
  • Specifications: Include the processor, RAM, storage type and capacity, and graphics if applicable.
  • Working condition: Describe the screen, keyboard, ports, battery, hinges and any faults or repairs.
  • Accessories and locks: Mention the charger and other included accessories, and disclose any account or device-management lock.

Clear photos and honest descriptions help the estimator compare like with like. Avoid including passwords or sensitive information in your listing.
