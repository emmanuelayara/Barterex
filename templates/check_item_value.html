What decides what a used phone is worth

  • Model and storage. Newer flagship models and larger storage sizes hold their value best.
  • Screen and body condition. Cracks, deep scratches and dents lower the value; a clean screen and frame raise it.
  • Battery health. A phone that lasts a full day is worth more than one that needs charging every few hours.
  • Age and usage. Tell us how long you have used it. Fairly used items need a usage duration.
  • Locks and repairs. Network, iCloud or Google account locks and non-original repairs reduce value, so be upfront about them.
  • Box and accessories. The original charger, cable and box make a phone easier to trade.

The same logic applies to laptops, consoles, TVs, footwear and other items: condition, age and current market demand set the price.
