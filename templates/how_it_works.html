  1. Create your account

    Sign up for free with your email. Complete your profile with a photo and description. Verify your account to start trading.

  2. List your items

    Add items you want to trade. Include clear photos, descriptions, and condition details. Your item stays with you - nothing is shipped or handed over at this stage. Our team reviews your listing.

  3. Get valued & earn provisional BXC

    Our AI-powered system analyzes your item's images and description to estimate fair market value. Once you accept the valuation, provisional BXC are added to your account. They can be used to reserve items, and are confirmed when your item passes physical verification.

  4. Browse & discover

    Explore items you can reserve using your BXC. Search by category, condition, or keywords to find what you're looking for in our marketplace.

  5. Reserve & verify

    Reserve an item with your BXC, backed by your own items. Within the 72-hour settlement window, the items involved are physically verified at a pickup station (free) or through our home service (quoted fee, paid by you). Barter Express does not hold your items before this point.

  6. Receive & rate

    Once verification passes, collect your item at the pickup station or receive it through the home service. Confirm receipt and rate your experience to help the community.
