How does trading by barter work?

  1. Check an item’s value

    Describe and photograph an item you own. You can start with a private estimate before deciding whether to create an account or trade.

  2. Explore and reserve

    Browse approved marketplace listings and use Barter Express Credits (BXC), including eligible item-backed credits, to reserve an item.

  3. Verify and collect

    Choose an available verification centre in your state. Required items are inspected before final acceptance, service-fee payment and collection at the centre.

Swapping across Nigeria

Barter Express serves users nationwide. Browse the marketplace by state to see listed items; listings and verification-centre availability can vary by location. Check the available centres when making a reservation.

Items stay with their owners until needed for verification. A valuation is an estimate, not a guarantee of a swap. See how it works for the steps and fees.
