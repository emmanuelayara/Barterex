10. Exchanges, BXC and Fees

Non-Custody Principle

Barter Express is a valuation, verification and exchange platform. We do not take possession of, store, or hold your items while they are valued, submitted for a trade request, pre-verified, or activated on the marketplace. You keep your items, and remain responsible for them, until a reservation requires physical verification.

BXC (Barter Express Credits)

  • BXC are platform credits, not money, a bank deposit, or e-money. They cannot be withdrawn, refunded, or transferred to external accounts.
  • BXC granted after you accept an AI valuation are provisional. They can be used only to reserve items, and are confirmed once your item passes physical verification.
  • When you reserve an item, the BXC involved are reserved against that trade and are released or applied depending on the outcome of verification.

Reservations, Verification and Backing Assets

  • When you reserve a marketplace item, you choose one or more of your own activated items as backing assets to cover its value.
  • The seller's item and each backing asset must pass physical verification within the 72-hour settlement window. Failing to submit items in time, or submitting items that fail inspection, may reduce your reliability score and cancel the reservation.
  • By selecting an item as a backing asset you consent to the following: once it passes physical verification, ownership of that item transfers to Barter Express and it is recorded in our inventory. Your consent is recorded at the time you confirm the reservation.
  • Backing assets are locked from the marketplace and cannot back any other reservation until the reservation resolves.

Verification and Delivery

  • Physical verification takes place at a Barter Express pickup station (free), or through the optional home service.
  • The home-service fee is quoted upfront, must be paid by you before pickup and verification, and is non-refundable.
  • Items in transit through our courier partners are insured under those partners' cover. Compensation for loss or damage is subject to that cover and our dispute process.
  • You are responsible for the accurate condition and description of your items at the time of verification and handover.

Fees

  • Signing up and listing items is free.
  • Your first AI valuation each day is free. Each additional valuation costs ₦100.
  • A transaction fee of 3% of the exchange value (minimum ₦500, maximum ₦5,000) applies only when an exchange successfully completes, not when an item is merely requested or reserved. It is not deducted automatically from your BXC or account. You pay it directly to the company at our office by bank transfer or other available payment methods.
  • Fees are shown before you pay them.

Disputes

Disputes should be reported within 7 days of the exchange. Barter Express will review verification and delivery records and may mediate, adjust BXC, compensate you under applicable courier cover, or reverse the exchange.
